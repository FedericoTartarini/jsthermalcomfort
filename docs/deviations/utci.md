## Notes

- `src/models/utci.ts` `_utci_optimized`: upstream's kernel runs under numba, and the library's equals it bit for bit on 200,000 random cases fed upstream's own `pa` (tdb from [-60, 60) °C, tr - tdb from [-40, 80) °C, v from [0, 20) m/s, rh from [-5, 105) %, half of them rounded to one decimal; numba 0.68.0 in the parity image, node v26.8.2).
- `src/models/utci.ts` `utci`: upstream's nested `exponential` runs through numpy, whose `np.log`, `np.power` and `np.exp` call the C library's, and the library's `Math` is V8's own (ADR 0009). On the 200,000 cases' `tk`, `Math.exp` differs from `np.exp` on 20,841 of numpy's arguments and `Math.log` from `np.log` on 2,548; `Math.pow(tk, n)` differs from `np.power` on 481, 297, 164 and 301 for n = -2, -1, 3 and 4 and on none for 0, 1 and 2, and `1 / tk` equals `np.power(tk, -1)` on all of them, so the -1 term is `1 / tk` (numpy 2.5.4). The exp, log and pow differences are accepted: ADR 0009 takes `Math.pow` for numpy's `pow`, and accepts `Math.exp` and `Math.log` differing by an ulp. With the gate off and `round_output` false, the unrounded utci equals upstream's on 190,919 of the 200,000 cases, and 24 differ by more than the parity suite's default tolerance, the worst by 6.6 times it, at tdb 59.67 °C, tr 26.34 °C, v 15.21 m/s and rh 43.34 %, where utci is 0.386; rounded, all 200,000 agree. In the parity data, 365 of the 377 unrounded cases with a finite value are bit-identical, the others within 2.02e-12, and the 351 rounded cases agree exactly.
- `src/models/utci.ts` `_utci_optimized`: CPython 3.12 folds each of the kernel's 166 `(c * (10 ** (-k)))` into one constant before numba compiles it: no `**` is left in its bytecode, every constant equals `c * 1e-k`, and `10 ** (-k)` is the literal `1e-k` for k = 3 to 13. The library writes `c * 1e-k`, where it wrote `c * 10 ** -k`: `10 ** -4` and `10 ** -5`, which are `Math.pow`, are an ulp below `1e-4` and `1e-5` on Node 22.13.0 and 22.23.4 (V8 12.4) and equal them on 24.21.0 and 26.8.2, which put parity case 922 1.1e-11 from upstream on Node 22, beyond the default tolerance; every other `Math` call of `utci` at that case gives the same bits on the four. With the literals the kernel equals upstream's on all of 200,000 further cases drawn as above (seed 20261028) on the four Node versions, where `c * 10 ** -k` equalled it on 62,936 on Node 22, at most 1.9e-10 apart. The full unrounded utci, gate off, equals upstream's on 190,733 of those cases on Node 22 and 190,760 on Node 24 and 26, and 29 differ by more than the default tolerance on each, the worst by 16.4 times it, at tdb 49.37860597781474 °C, tr 67.6126232175213 °C, v 1.5984902002188583 m/s and rh 104.00326723324535 %, where upstream gives -0.8843569114596903, through `exponential`'s `Math.exp`, `Math.log` and `Math.pow`.
- `src/models/utci.ts` `utci`: upstream's `np.asarray` calls and its `np.where` on `all_valid` have no counterpart, since the model takes scalars (ADR 0019, whitelist items 8 and 9). `units_converter(tdb=tdb, tr=tr, v=v)` takes `from_units`' default, IP, which the library's positional call `units_converter(Units.IP, { tdb, tr, v })` passes explicitly, and `units_converter(tmp=utci_approx, from_units=Units.SI.value.lower())[0]` passes 'si', which `units_converter` upper-cases (`utilities.py:133`), where the library passes `Units.SI`.
- `src/models/utci.ts` `UTCIInputs`: `units` takes `Units.IP` or `"IP"` (ADR 0019, whitelist item 5). Upstream's `utci(tdb=77, tr=77, v=3.28, rh=50, units=Units.IP)` raises `AttributeError: 'Units' object has no attribute 'upper'`, since its `Units` members are not strings; the library's `Units.IP` is the string `"IP"`.
- `src/models/utci.ts` `UTCI_INFO`: `units` is `BASE_INPUTS_UNITS` (`src/_internal/define.ts`), the entry `CLO_TOUT_INFO` also references, since upstream writes its default and check once, in `BaseInputs` (`classes_input.py:95, :114-121`).
