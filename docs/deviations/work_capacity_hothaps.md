## Notes

- `src/models/work_capacity_hothaps.ts` `work_capacity_hothaps`: upstream's `(wbgt / divisor) ** exponent` is numpy's float64 power, the C library's `pow`, where the library's `**` is V8's own (ADR 0009); `np.clip` is `Math.max` and `Math.min` (ADR 0019, whitelist item 9), and the rest is float64 arithmetic in upstream's order. On 100,000 `wbgt` drawn from [0, 50) °C (numpy's `default_rng(42)`), half of them rounded to one decimal, and 607 chosen points (0.0 to 60.0 °C by 0.1, the three divisors, 1e-300, 1e300 and -0.0 °C), each with the three intensities, `capacity` equals upstream's on 301,808 of 301,821 and differs by at most 3.7e-16 relative on the others (numpy 2.5.4 in the parity image, node v26.8.2). In the parity data, the 965 finite cases are bit-identical and the 35 NaN cases are NaN.
- `src/models/work_capacity_hothaps.ts` `work_capacity_hothaps`: upstream's `np.asarray(wbgt)` has no counterpart, since the model takes scalars (ADR 0019, whitelist item 8). `WorkIntensity(work_intensity.lower())` is `work_intensity.toLowerCase()` (item 4), since `WorkIntensity`'s values are the strings themselves (deviation _shared-77), and `params` is keyed by them, as upstream's is by the members. A negative `wbgt` gives NaN, as upstream's does, `null` here (decision 310); numpy's `RuntimeWarning` "invalid value encountered in scalar power" has no counterpart (decision 86).
