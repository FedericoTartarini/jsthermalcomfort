## Notes

- `src/models/at.ts` `at`: upstream's `psy_ta_rh(tdb, rh).p_vap` carries `p_sat`'s `np.exp` and `np.log`, where the library's `Math.exp` and `Math.log` are V8's own (ADR 0009; `_shared.md`'s Notes on `p_sat`); the rest of the formula is float64 arithmetic in the order upstream writes it. On 100,000 (`tdb`, `rh`, `v`) drawn from [-50, 60) °C, [0, 100) % and [0, 30) m/s, with `q` from [-200, 1200) W/m² in half of them, half of the values rounded to one decimal, and 21 chosen points (`tdb` -243.12, -50, 0, 60 and 100 °C at `rh` 0 and 100, with and without `q`, and `v` -10 m/s), the unrounded `at` equals upstream's on 98,448 of 100,021, with the same 11 NaN and infinite positions, and differs by at most 4.8e-13 °C (1.9e-13 relative) on the others; after `round(t_at, 1)` every value equals upstream's (numpy 2.5.4 in the parity image, node v26.8.2). In the parity data, 489 of the 500 unrounded cases are bit-identical and all 500 rounded ones equal.
- `src/models/at.ts` `at`: upstream's `np.asarray` calls on `tdb`, `rh`, `v` and `q`, with the comment above the first three, "Convert lists to numpy arrays if necessary", have no counterpart, since the model takes scalars (ADR 0019, whitelist item 8), and `psy_ta_rh(tdb, rh)` takes the helper's default `p_atm`, as upstream's call does. Upstream's `4.00` reads `4.0`, as Prettier prints it. A `v` of -10 m/s divides by zero in `0.7 * q / (v + 10)`, giving ±Infinity or NaN, as upstream's numpy division does, the NaN `null` here (decision 310); an `rh` of 0 gives a finite `at`, since `p_vap` is 0, while `psy_ta_rh`'s dew point takes `log(0)`, as upstream's does; numpy's `RuntimeWarning`s at both have no counterpart (decision 86). An `rh` outside [0, 100] throws `dew_point_tmp`'s `RangeError` from inside `psy_ta_rh` (deviation _shared-57), after the parse, where upstream raises after `ATInputs`, so the order of the checks is upstream's.
- `src/models/at.ts` `ATInputs`: upstream's `ATInputs` passes `v` to `BaseInputs` as `vr` (`classes_input.py:298`), so its type error names `vr` ("vr must be one of the following types: …", measured on 4.7.0 with `v="0.1"`); the parse's message names the key the caller gave, `v` (deviation _shared-10). `q` is a `numeric` entry whose default is `null`, upstream's `None` (decision 252): left out or `null`, it picks the formula without solar load, as upstream's `q is not None` does (`models/at.py:71-75`).
