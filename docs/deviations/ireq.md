## Notes

- `src/models/ireq.ts` `_solve_single_criterion`: upstream's kernel runs under numba, which unrolls a power with an integer literal exponent into multiplications (`static_power_impl`, `numba/cpython/numbers.py`, numba 0.68.0), so `x**4` is `(x * x) * (x * x)` and `x**3` is `x * (x * x)`, and lowers `p**0.075` to the C library's `pow`, which `**` stands for; `math.exp` and `math.log` are the C library's, `Math.exp` and `Math.log` V8's (ADR 0009). On 100,000 cases drawn with numpy's `default_rng(42)` from the parity generator's ranges, half of each value rounded to one decimal, and 13 chosen points (the mirrored tests' boundary cases, `tr` and `tdb` of ±1e300, `vr` 25 and 0 m/s, `p` 1e-300, `tdb` -235 and -1320 °C), 3 raise "division by zero" in both (deviation ireq-6). On the other 100,010, with each of the four `limit_inputs` and `round_output` combinations, every rounded output and every `dle` equals upstream's bit for bit, the unrounded `ireq` and `icl` equal it on 90,895 to 92,909 with the gate off, the others at most 4.8e-8 clo (3.8e-10 relative) apart, and on the kernel's own arguments V8's `Math.exp` differs from numba's on 45,330 of 500,050, `Math.log` on 3,694 of 100,010 `p` and `p ** 0.075` on 76 (node v26.8.2). In the parity data, 963 of the 1,000 cases are bit-identical, and the other 37 differ only in an unrounded `ireq` or `icl`, within the suite's tolerance.
- `src/models/ireq.ts` `ireq`: the docstring's citation `[11079ISO2007]_` links to `docs/references.md`, its `:py:class:` reference is `{@link IREQ}`, its `.. note::` is `> [!NOTE]`, and its References section is `@see` (ADR 0025); the rest of the docstring is kept verbatim, Python's terms included.
- `src/models/ireq.ts` `ireq`: upstream's `is_scalar`, `_scalar` and `np.vectorize` of `_solve_single_criterion` as `_solve_ireq_criterion` have no counterpart, since the model takes scalars (ADR 0019, whitelist items 8 and 9), so the body calls `_solve_single_criterion` itself. `IREQInputs(…)` is the parse, then `IREQInputs.__post_init__`'s seven checks (`classes_input.py:1361-1374`) as inline throws with upstream's messages, in its order; its broadcast check has no counterpart. `results` is an object keyed as upstream's dict, and the masks are one ternary per output.
- `src/models/ireq.ts` `ireq`: the non-physical check reads the `ireq_*` and `icl_*` rows' `_valid_range` with `Object.is`, so a NaN output, which `_valid_range` returns unchanged and upstream's `< 0` does not flag, masks nothing, as upstream's does not.
- `src/models/ireq.ts` `_valid_iso_11079_inputs`: upstream's mask becomes four `_valid_range` calls, one per quantity, which append the rows and whose results give the mask; `walk_sp`'s bound is built per call, `min(0.0052 * (met - 58.0), 1.2)` written as upstream writes it, the `max` read from `IREQ_INFO`. `IREQ_INFO.inputs.walk_sp.applicability` holds no `min`, since a `Bound` holds fixed numbers and this one depends on the call's `met` (decision 265).
- `src/models/ireq.ts` `_format_dle`: upstream's object array, filled where `unlimited` and where not, is one value: "more than 8", or the hours, rounded with the library's `round` (ADR 0019, whitelist item 7) where `round_output` is true.
