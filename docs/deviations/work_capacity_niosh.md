## Notes

- `src/models/work_capacity_niosh.ts` `work_capacity_niosh`: upstream's arithmetic is float64 subtraction, multiplication and division, in upstream's order, which V8 computes alike, and `np.log10`, the C library's, where the library's `Math.log10` is V8's own (ADR 0009). `np.clip(capacity, 0, 100)` is written as the comparisons `capacity < 0 ? 0 : capacity > 100 ? 100 : capacity`, as `work_capacity_iso`'s Notes give, since numpy keeps a -0.0 that is not below 0. On 100,000 inputs drawn from `wbgt` in [0, 50) °C and `met` in [0, 2500) W (numpy's `default_rng(42)`), half of them rounded to one decimal, and 8,126 chosen points (16 `met` values: 0, -0.0, 5e-324, 1e-300, 1, 10, 50, 100, the resting 117, 200, 300, 500, 1000, 1500, 2000 and 2500 W, each with 507 `wbgt` values: 0.0 to 50.0 °C by 0.1, the resting limit, ±0.0, 1e-300 and ±1e300, and each finite limit's own `wbgt`), `Math.log10` equals `np.log10` on 71,372 of the 71,696 distinct `met` values, differs by one ulp on 323 and by two on one (`met` 1.741392436126199 W), and gives `np.log10(117)`'s bits; `capacity` equals upstream's bit for bit on 108,073 of 108,126, the sign of zero and the 2 NaN cases included, and differs by at most 1.3e-13 (5.2e-15 relative) on the other 53 (numpy 2.5.4 in the parity image, node v26.8.2). Of the 677 -0.0 cases, 671 have `met` 0 or -0.0 W and `wbgt` at or below the resting limit, where the quotient is a non-negative number over -inf, and 6 have `wbgt` exactly on the resting limit, `56.7 - 11.5 * np.log10(117)`, with `met` above 0 and below 117 W. In the parity data, the 1,000 cases are bit-identical, the 54 at 117 W and the 36 at 0 W included.
- `src/models/work_capacity_niosh.ts` `work_capacity_niosh`: upstream's `np.asarray(wbgt)` and `np.asarray(met)` have no counterpart, since the model takes scalars (ADR 0019, whitelist item 8). At 0 W, `np.log10` and `Math.log10` give -inf, the limit is +inf, and `capacity` is -0.0 at `wbgt` at or below the resting limit and 0 above it, as upstream's are. At the resting 117 W the two limits coincide and the division is by zero: `capacity` is 100 or 0 after the clip, and NaN at `wbgt` on the limit, as upstream's are. numpy's `RuntimeWarning`s "divide by zero encountered in log10", "divide by zero encountered in scalar divide" and "invalid value encountered in scalar divide" have no counterpart (decision 86).
- `src/models/work_capacity_niosh.ts` `work_capacity_niosh`: `WorkCapacityStandardsInputs(wbgt=wbgt, met=met)` is the parse and `checkWorkCapacityStandardsInputs` (`src/_internal/define.ts`), which holds the class's `met` check (`classes_input.py:1095-1099`) once for `work_capacity_iso` and this model (decision 257).
- `src/models/work_capacity_niosh.ts` `work_capacity_niosh`: the inputs type is `WorkCapacityStandardsInputs` (`classes_input.py:1083`), which `src/models/work_capacity_iso.ts` declares, the first of the two models upstream validates with it migrated (decision 256); `InputsOf<typeof WORK_CAPACITY_NIOSH_INFO>` is the same type, which a type test checks. The model returns `WorkCapacity`, which `src/models/work_capacity_dunne.ts` declares (decision 122).
