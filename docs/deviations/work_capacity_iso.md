## Notes

- `src/models/work_capacity_iso.ts` `work_capacity_iso`: upstream's arithmetic is float64 subtraction, division and multiplication, in upstream's order, which V8 computes alike (ADR 0009). `np.clip(capacity, 0, 100)` is written as the comparisons `capacity < 0 ? 0 : capacity > 100 ? 100 : capacity`, not `Math.max` and `Math.min`, since numpy keeps a -0.0 that is not below 0 (measured: `np.clip(-0.0, 0, 100)` is -0.0) where `Math.max(-0, 0)` is 0, as `two_nodes_gagge`'s Notes write numba's `max` and `min`; a NaN passes through both forms. On 100,000 inputs drawn from `wbgt` in [0, 50) °C and `met` in [0, 2500) W (numpy's `default_rng(42)`), half of them rounded to one decimal, and 6,156 chosen points (12 `met` values from 0 to 2500 W, with -0.0, 1e-300 and the resting 117 W, each with 513 `wbgt` values: 0.0 to 50.0 °C by 0.1, both limits, ±0.0, 1e-300 and ±1e300), `capacity` equals upstream's bit for bit on all 106,156, the sign of zero and the 2 NaN cases included (numpy 2.5.4 in the parity image, node v26.8.2); the -0.0 cases are `wbgt` exactly on the resting limit, `34.9 - 117 / 46`, with `met` below 117 W. In the parity data, the 1,000 cases are bit-identical, the 52 at 117 W included.
- `src/models/work_capacity_iso.ts` `work_capacity_iso`: upstream's `np.asarray(wbgt)` and `np.asarray(met)` have no counterpart, since the model takes scalars (ADR 0019, whitelist item 8). At the resting 117 W the two limits coincide and the division is by zero: `capacity` is 100 or 0 after the clip, and NaN at `wbgt` on the limit, as upstream's are; numpy's `RuntimeWarning`s "divide by zero encountered in scalar divide" and "invalid value encountered in scalar divide" have no counterpart (decision 86).
- `src/models/work_capacity_iso.ts` `work_capacity_iso`: `WorkCapacityStandardsInputs(wbgt=wbgt, met=met)` is the parse and `checkWorkCapacityStandardsInputs` (`src/_internal/define.ts`), which holds the class's `met` check (`classes_input.py:1095-1099`) once for this model and `work_capacity_niosh` (decision 257).
- `src/models/work_capacity_iso.ts` `WorkCapacityStandardsInputs`: the type takes upstream's name, `WorkCapacityStandardsInputs` (`classes_input.py:1083`), which upstream validates this model and `work_capacity_niosh` with (`models/work_capacity_iso.py:46`, `models/work_capacity_niosh.py:46`); it is declared here, the first of the two migrated (decision 256). The model returns `WorkCapacity`, which `src/models/work_capacity_dunne.ts` declares (decision 122).
