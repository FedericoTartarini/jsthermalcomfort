## Notes

- `src/models/net.ts` `net`: upstream's `v**0.75` is numpy's float64 power on a 0-d array, the C library's `pow`, where the library's `v ** 0.75` is V8's own (ADR 0009); the rest is float64 arithmetic in upstream's order. On 100,000 (`tdb`, `rh`, `v`) drawn from [-40, 60) °C, [0, 100) % and [0, 30) m/s (numpy's `default_rng(42)`), half of them rounded to one decimal, and 105 chosen points (`tdb` -40, 0, 25, 37 and 60 °C, `rh` 0, 50 and 100 %, `v` 0, -0.0, 5e-324, 1e-12, 0.1, 30 and 1e300 m/s), `v ** 0.75` differs from numpy's on 217 of the 100,105 `v`, the unrounded `net` equals upstream's bit for bit on 100,101 and differs by at most 1.4e-14 °C on the other 4, as it does with `Math.pow(v, 0.75)`, and the rounded `net` equals upstream's on all (numpy 2.5.4 in the parity image, node v26.8.2). In the parity data, all 503 unrounded and all 497 rounded cases are bit-identical.
- `src/models/net.ts` `net`: upstream's `np.asarray(tdb)`, `np.asarray(rh)` and `np.asarray(v)` have no counterpart, since the model takes scalars (ADR 0019, whitelist item 8). Upstream checks no range, so an `rh` outside [0, 100] gives a `net`, and a negative `v` gives NaN, as upstream's do (measured on 4.7.0: `rh` -10 and 150 at `tdb` 30 °C and `v` 1 m/s give 20.50243254077619 and 32.44930790278448, `v` -1 m/s gives NaN, `null` here, decision 310); numpy's `RuntimeWarning` "invalid value encountered in power" has no counterpart (decision 86).
