## Notes

- `src/models/thi.ts` `thi`: upstream's arithmetic is float64 multiplication, addition and subtraction in upstream's order, which V8 computes alike (ADR 0009). On 100,000 (`tdb`, `rh`) drawn from [-50, 60) °C and [0, 100) % (numpy's `default_rng(42)`), half of them rounded to one decimal, and 66 chosen points (`tdb` -50, -40, 0, -0.0, 20, 25, 30, 60, 1e300, -1e300 and 5e-324 °C, `rh` 0, -0.0, 5e-324, 50, 70 and 100 %), the unrounded `thi` equals upstream's bit for bit on all 100,066, and the rounded one equals on all (numpy 2.5.4 in the parity image, node v26.8.2). In the parity data, all 1,000 cases are bit-identical.
- `src/models/thi.ts` `thi`: upstream's `np.asarray(tdb)` and `np.asarray(rh)` have no counterpart, since the model takes scalars (ADR 0019, whitelist item 8). `THIInputs(…)` is the parse, then `THIInputs.__post_init__`'s range check (`classes_input.py:931-933`) as an inline throw with upstream's message, as decision 238 keeps each `rh` check, and `np.any(rh < 0) or np.any(rh > 100)` is one comparison.
