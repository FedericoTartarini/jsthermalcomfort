## Notes

- `src/models/two_nodes_gagge_sleep.ts` `_sleep_set_optimized`: upstream's kernel runs under numba, which unrolls a power with an integer literal exponent into multiplications (`static_power_impl`, `numba/cpython/numbers.py`, numba 0.68.0), so `x ** 3` is `x * (x * x)`, and `_two_nodes_gagge_sleep_optimized`'s `hour**5`, `hour**4`, `hour**3` and `hour**2` are `hour * (hour * hour * (hour * hour))`, `hour * hour * (hour * hour)`, `hour * (hour * hour)` and `hour * hour`; `10**-8` is `1.0 / 10**8`, which equals `10 ** -8`, 1e-8. It lowers `x ** 0.5`, `v**-0.29`, `v**-0.08`, `temp_diff**0.2740`, `v**0.1242` and `(met - 0.85) ** 0.39` to the C library's `pow`, so they are `Math.pow`, and `math.exp` is the C library's, so `Math.exp` (ADR 0009). Measured in the parity image: the unrolled forms equal numba's powers on all of 2,000 values of `hour` and 20,000 bases from 270 to 320, where `Math.pow` equals numba's `hour**5` on 997 and its cube on 14,164; numba's `x ** 0.5` equals `Math.pow(x, 0.5)` and `Math.sqrt(x)` on all of 20,000 values of `(height * weight) / 3600`. V8's `Math.exp` differs from the C library's `exp` on 1,894 of 20,000 arguments from -10 to 5, and `Math.pow` on 27 of 15,000 with these exponents (node v26.8.2). The Newton loops call `exp` many times per minute, so in the parity data 355 of the 1,000 cases are bit-identical in every element, and the 2,908 of 74,210 elements that differ are at most 1.4e-13 relative apart (pythermalcomfort 4.8.0).
- `src/models/two_nodes_gagge_sleep.ts` `_sleep_set_optimized`: numba's `max(a, b)` returns `a` unless `b > a`, and `min(a, b)` returns `a` unless `b < a`, so each is written as that comparison, as `two_nodes_gagge_ji`'s register Notes give them: `max(3, x)` is `x > 3 ? x : 3`, and `min(max(skin_blood_flow, 0.5), 90)` two comparisons in that order. A zero divisor is checked before the division, with the divisor written as upstream writes it, where it can be zero for finite inputs (row two_nodes_gagge_sleep-4); numba raises for -0.0 too, which `=== 0` matches. `skin_blood_flow + 0.585417`, whose `skin_blood_flow` is at least 0.5, `tbm_h - tbm_l` in the branch where `tbm_l <= t_body < tbm_h`, and the first Newton loop's `err_diff`, which upstream checks against 1e-10 first, cannot be zero, and the other divisors are nonzero constants.
