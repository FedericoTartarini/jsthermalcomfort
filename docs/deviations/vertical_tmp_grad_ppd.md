## Notes

- `src/models/vertical_tmp_grad_ppd.ts` `vertical_tmp_grad_ppd`: upstream's `np.exp` runs on a 0-d array through numpy, which calls the C library's `exp`, and the library's `Math.exp` is V8's own (ADR 0009). `tsv` is the inner `pmv_ppd_ashrae`'s pmv, rounded to two decimals as its `round_output` default rounds it, whose arithmetic its register notes; it is a numpy float64 scalar, whose `** 2` is the C library's `pow`, and on every two-decimal `tsv` from -30 to 30 `(tsv - 1.91) ** 2` equals `(tsv - 1.91) * (tsv - 1.91)` (6,001 of 6,001, numpy 2.5.4 in the parity image), as V8's `** 2` does on 2,000,000 random doubles (node v26.8.2), so the library keeps `** 2`. On the 1,000 parity cases, the 326 rounded finite `ppd_vg` equal upstream's, and 284 of the 293 unrounded finite ones are bit-identical, the others at most 1.8e-15 relative apart (node v26.8.2); 242 finite cases are the clamp's 0.
- `src/models/vertical_tmp_grad_ppd.ts` `vertical_tmp_grad_ppd`: upstream's `np.asarray` calls have no counterpart, since the model takes scalars (ADR 0019, whitelist items 8 and 9); `np.maximum` is `Math.max`, `np.round` the library's `round`, and the `np.where` calls on `all_valid` ternaries. The acceptability threshold, `ppd_val <= 5`, stays inline, as upstream writes it, and is computed before `round_output` rounds `ppd_val`, as upstream's is.
- `src/models/vertical_tmp_grad_ppd.ts` `VERTICAL_TMP_GRAD_PPD_INFO`: `vr` is checked only as `v_limited`, on 0-0.2 m/s (`_internal/ashrae55.py:100-101`), since upstream passes `v_limited=vr` and no `v` (`models/vertical_tmp_grad_ppd.py:140-146`), so a `vr` row's key is `"v_limited"`. `rh` and `vertical_tmp_grad` have no applicability, as upstream checks neither.
