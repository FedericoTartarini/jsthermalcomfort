## Notes

- `src/models/clo_tout.ts` `clo_tout`: upstream's `np.power(10, -0.1635 - 0.0066 * tout)` runs numpy's float64 power on a 0-d array, and the library's `Math.pow` is V8's own. Unrounded, they differ on 358 of 300,000 `tout` drawn from [5, 26) (numpy 2.5.4 in the parity image, node v26.8.2), and after `np.around(clo, 2)` and the library's `round(clo, 2)` they differ on none (ADR 0009); the 1,000 parity cases agree exactly.
- `src/models/clo_tout.ts` `clo_tout`: upstream's `tout = np.asarray(tout)` and its comment "Convert tout to NumPy array for vectorized operations" have no counterpart, since a 0-d array is a number here (ADR 0019, whitelist item 8). `units_converter(tmp=tout)[0]` takes `from_units`' default, IP, which the library's positional call `units_converter(Units.IP, { tmp: tout })` passes explicitly.
- `src/models/clo_tout.ts` `CloTOutInputs`: `units` takes `Units.IP` or `"IP"` (ADR 0019, whitelist item 5). Upstream's `clo_tout(tout=20, units=Units.IP)` raises `AttributeError: 'Units' object has no attribute 'upper'` at `models/clo_tout.py:73`, since its `Units` members are not strings; the library's `Units.IP` is the string `"IP"`.
