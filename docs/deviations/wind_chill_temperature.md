## Notes

- `src/models/wind_chill_temperature.ts` `wind_chill_temperature`: upstream's `v**0.16` is numpy's float64 power on a 0-d array, the C library's `pow`, where the library's `v ** 0.16` is V8's own (ADR 0009). On 100,000 (`tdb`, `v`) drawn from [-60, 20) °C and [0, 120) km/h, every second one rounded to one decimal, and 8 chosen points (`v` 0, 1e-12 and 200 km/h, `tdb` -50 and 20 °C), V8's power differs from numpy's by one ulp on 110 of the 100,008 `v`, which moves the unrounded `wct` on 76 of them by at most 2.8e-14 °C (2.7e-14 relative), and the rounded `wct` equals upstream's on all 100,008; `Math.pow(v, 0.16)` gives the same values (numpy 2.5.4 in the parity image, node v26.8.2). In the parity data, all 533 unrounded and all 467 rounded cases equal upstream's.
- `src/models/wind_chill_temperature.ts` `wind_chill_temperature`: upstream's `np.asarray(tdb)` and `np.asarray(v)` have no counterpart, since the model takes scalars (ADR 0019, whitelist item 8). A negative `v` gives NaN, as upstream's does, `null` here (decision 310); numpy's `RuntimeWarning` "invalid value encountered in power" has no counterpart (decision 86).
- `src/models/wind_chill_temperature.ts` `wind_chill_temperature`: the summary's and the validation sentence's reStructuredText hyperlink references `` `WCT`_ `` and `` `Calculator.net`_ `` are Markdown links to the targets the docstring defines, and the two target lines go.
