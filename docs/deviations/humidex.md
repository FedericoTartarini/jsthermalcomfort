## Notes

- `src/models/humidex.ts` `humidex`: upstream's `10 ** (7.5 * tdb / (237.7 + tdb))` is numpy's float64 power on a 0-d array, and its `np.exp` and `dew_point_tmp`'s `np.exp` and `np.log` are the C library's, where the library's `**`, `Math.exp` and `Math.log` are V8's own (ADR 0009). On 100,000 (`tdb`, `rh`) drawn from [-50, 60) °C and [0, 100) %, half of them rounded to one decimal, and 6 chosen points (`rh` 0 and 100, `tdb` -243.12, 0 and 100 °C), the unrounded `humidex` equals upstream's on 99,947 under 'rana' and on 97,376 under 'masterson', with the same NaN and infinite positions, and differs by at most 4.3e-14 °C and 4.0e-13 °C on the others; after `round(hi, 1)` every value equals upstream's, and every `discomfort` equals (numpy 2.5.4 in the parity image, node v26.8.2). In the parity data, 495 of the 503 unrounded cases are bit-identical and all 497 rounded ones equal.
- `src/models/humidex.ts` `humidex`: upstream's `np.asarray(tdb)` and `np.asarray(rh)` have no counterpart, since the model takes scalars (ADR 0019, whitelist item 8), and `np.any(rh > 100) or np.any(rh < 0)` is one comparison. `stress_category`, `np.full_like(hi, "Heat stroke probable", dtype=object)` followed by five masked assignments, is that string followed by five `if`s in upstream's order (item 9), so a NaN `humidex`, which the 'masterson' model gives at `rh` 0, where `dew_point_tmp` takes `log(0)`, has "Heat stroke probable", as upstream's has, and a `humidex` of `null` (decision 310); numpy's `RuntimeWarning`s there have no counterpart (decision 86).
- `src/models/humidex.ts` `humidex`: the parse's `parseChoice` accepts a `model` in any case (`src/_internal/define.ts`), so the body keeps upstream's case-sensitive `model not in HumidexModels._value2member_map_` as a test of `HUMIDEX_INFO.inputs.model.allowed`, and 'Masterson' throws there, as upstream raises (decision 237). `model` keeps upstream's name: whitelist item 3's `standard` names an edition of a standard (decision 160), and `HumidexModels` holds two formulas (decision 236).
- `src/models/humidex.ts` `humidex`: the `model` note's reStructuredText hyperlink reference `` `Rana et al. (2013)`_ `` is a Markdown link to the target the docstring defines, and the target line goes, as `cooling_effect`'s are.
