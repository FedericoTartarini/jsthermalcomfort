## Notes

- `src/models/solar_gain.ts` `_solar_gain_scalar`, `_projected_area_factor`: upstream's kernel runs under numba, and its one libm call is `math.sin`. With `round_output` false, the unrounded `erf` and `delta_mrt` equal upstream's bit for bit on 362 of the 370 parity cases whose altitude and sharp are in the table, and the other 8 differ by at most 2.85e-14, 6 of them supine, whose angles come from `transpose_sharp_altitude`'s libm calls unrounded (`_shared.md` Notes); the 500 cases with `round_output` true agree exactly (pythermalcomfort 4.8.0 and numba 0.68.0 in the parity image, node v26.8.2; ADR 0009).
- `src/models/solar_gain.ts` `solar_gain`: upstream's posture codes (`_POSTURE_STANDING`, `_POSTURE_SITTING`, `_POSTURE_SUPINE`, `_POSTURE_CODES`), with their comment "integer codes for posture, since numba nopython mode can't dispatch on Python string/enum comparisons the way the rest of this module's helpers do", have no counterpart: `_projected_area_factor` and `_solar_gain_scalar` compare the lower-cased posture with `Postures`' strings (ADR 0019, whitelist item 9). `_solar_gain_array`, its `prange` loop, `np.broadcast_arrays` and the `np.asarray` calls have none either, since the model takes scalars (whitelist items 8 and 9).
- `src/models/solar_gain.ts` `solar_gain`: upstream's own posture check (`models/solar_gain.py:210-215`) runs in the parse, from `SOLAR_GAIN_INFO.inputs.posture` (decision 140), so before the `_valid_range` checks; a call that fails it throws either way. `posture.lower()` stays in the body, since the kernel reads the lower-cased posture.
- `src/models/solar_gain.ts` `SOLAR_GAIN_INFO.inputs.posture`: upstream's `solar_gain(..., posture=Postures.sitting)` raises "AttributeError: 'Postures' object has no attribute 'lower'", since its `Postures` members are not strings; the library's `Postures.sitting` is the string `"sitting"` (deviation `_shared-29`).
- `src/models/solar_gain.ts` `solar_gain`: the docstring's `` :py:meth:`pythermalcomfort.environment.f_svv` `` becomes the code span `f_svv`, the function's name in the library, where it is in `environment` (ADR 0025's `:py:` mapping).
